## Scenario:

The PSHS school canteen is small and often gets crowded during lunch break. Students line up to buy food, but the process is slow because:

-Some students take too long to decide what to order.
-The cashier has to manually calculate totals and give change.
-There is no system to track which food items are running out.

Main Problem: The school canten queue is too slow because payment, ordering, and food management are done manually.

### Sub-Problems:
1. Students take too long to decide what to order.
2. The cashier has to manually calculate totals and give change.
3. There is no system to track which food items are running out.
4. The queue becomes too crowded and takes too long to serve students.

### CT SKILL(S) NEEDED FOR THE PROBLEM:
1. Decomposition, Pattern Recognition, and Algorithmic Thinking
2. Algorithmic Thinking
3. Abstraction and Algorithmic Thinking
4. Decomposition

### Example Solution:
1. Create a menu that shows food names, pictures, and their prices so scanning can be more efficient and people can decide faster.
2. Create an ordering system wherein it can calculate the total price and change.
3. Create an inventory system that keeps track of available quantities and alerts the cashier when the stock is low/running out.
4. Organize the process into simpler steps. Make different stations where students can choose what they want, then order, then collect the food.

PSEUDOCODE:
START
Display menu and prices
Students order food
Calculate total price
Display total
Receive payment
Calculate change = payment - total
Display change
Update food stock
Complete order
END
